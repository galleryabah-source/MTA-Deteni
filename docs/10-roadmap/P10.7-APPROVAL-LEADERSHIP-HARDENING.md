# P10.7 — APPROVAL & LEADERSHIP HARDENING

Status: IMPLEMENTED / CONTRACT-VERIFIED / SCHEMA-PRESERVING

P10.7 binds APPROVED to explicit approval authority, scope, separation of duties, immutable evidence and revision.

## Approval chain

SUBJECT
→ AUTHORITY + SCOPE
→ APPROVAL DECISION
→ IMMUTABLE EVIDENCE
→ OPTIONAL SECOND APPROVAL
→ AUDIT
→ OUTBOX

## Invariants

- Approval identity and reason are mandatory.
- Approval authority and scope are both checked.
- Self second-approval is prohibited.
- Required second approval is enforced.
- Existing approval evidence cannot be rewritten.
- Approval revisions are sequential.
- Critical approval mutation remains behind P9 transaction/idempotency with audit and outbox.
- No production schema migration is introduced.

The contract does not claim live approval DB/HTTP E2E or audit/outbox delivery evidence.
