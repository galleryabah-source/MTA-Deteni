# P10.2 — PLACEMENT HARDENING

Status: IMPLEMENTED / CONTRACT-VERIFIED / SCHEMA-PRESERVING

P10.2 hardens placement as a domain boundary.

## Invariants

1. Detainee, block, room and bed identifiers are mandatory.
2. A detainee cannot receive a second active placement.
3. A bed cannot be occupied by another detainee.
4. The same detainee may safely reconcile against its own current bed occupant.
5. Optimistic version checks reject stale placement state.
6. Closing an active placement increments the version.
7. Placement authorization remains mandatory.
8. No production schema migration is introduced.

## Cross-domain invariant

DETAINEE
  ↓
PLACEMENT
  ↓
MOVEMENT / TRANSFER
  ↓
HEADCOUNT / MONITORING
  ↓
REPORTING

Critical placement mutations must continue through the P9 transaction/idempotency boundary, with audit and outbox emission.
