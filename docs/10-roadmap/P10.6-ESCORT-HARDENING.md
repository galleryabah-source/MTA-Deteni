# P10.6 — ESCORT HARDENING

Status: IMPLEMENTED / CONTRACT-VERIFIED / SCHEMA-PRESERVING

P10.6 binds ESCORT_ASSIGNED to actor authority, officer scope, temporary-exit state, movement evidence and optimistic concurrency.

## Operational chain

DOCUMENTED
  ↓
ESCORT_ASSIGNED
  ↓
ACTIVE
  ↓
MOVEMENT: TEMPORARY_EXIT_DEPARTURE
  ↓
MOVEMENT: TEMPORARY_EXIT_RETURN
  ↓
COMPLETED

## Invariants

- Escort assignment requires exit, actor and correlation identity.
- At least one unique escort officer is required.
- Assignment requires a DOCUMENTED temporary exit.
- Actor authorization and officer scope are both checked.
- An exit can have only one assignment through repository uniqueness checks.
- ACTIVE and COMPLETED require movement evidence.
- Optimistic versioning rejects stale concurrent changes.
- Critical mutation remains behind P9 transaction/idempotency with audit and outbox.
- No production schema migration is introduced.

The contract does not claim live personnel-directory, database, HTTP, movement, audit or outbox E2E evidence.
