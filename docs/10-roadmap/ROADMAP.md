## D9 — Pilot & Hardening
- pilot in controlled environment;
- security regression;
- performance testing;
- backup/restore drill;

## D10 — Production & Institutionalization
- production release;
- operational training;
- governance review;
- KPI monitoring;

## Delivery Rule

Advance a phase only when its acceptance evidence is complete. Never bypass security, authorization, data-governance, or audit gates merely to accelerate feature delivery.
