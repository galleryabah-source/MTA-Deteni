# MTA DETENI — P13.5721–5760 Disaster Recovery Evidence Binding

## Objective

Upgrade disaster-recovery rehearsal from checklist presence to execution-backed evidence.

## Required evidence per step

Every DR step must bind:

- `controlId`;
- `executionId`;
- commit SHA;
- environment;
- start/end timestamp;
- operator identity or controlled executor identity;
- backup identity where applicable;
- restore artifact identity where applicable;
- integrity result;
- replay/rebuild result where applicable;
- final step status.

## Mandatory sequence

```text
BACKUP_CREATED
  ↓
BACKUP_IDENTIFIED
  ↓
RESTORE_STARTED
  ↓
RESTORE_COMPLETED
  ↓
INTEGRITY_VERIFIED
  ↓
JOURNAL_REPLAYED
  ↓
PROJECTION_REBUILT
  ↓
RECONCILIATION_VERIFIED
  ↓
RECOVERY_ACCEPTED
```

## Acceptance rule

A named step without observable evidence is `NOT_RUN`, not PASS.

A failed integrity verification blocks `RECOVERY_ACCEPTED`.

A missing backup or restore identity blocks certification.

## Safety

The rehearsal is synthetic/non-production only while production authorization is FALSE and Migration Freeze is TRUE. No production restore, production migration, or real detainee data is permitted by this checkpoint.
