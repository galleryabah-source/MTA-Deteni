## Rules

1. Every official template has owner, version, effective date, status, and archive metadata.
2. Historical documents remain bound to the exact template version used at generation time.
3. Placeholder validation occurs before generation; unresolved required fields block issuance.
4. Numbering/register allocation is controlled and idempotent; retries cannot silently allocate conflicting official numbers.
5. Approval/signature metadata is recorded separately from document generation.
6. Document generation is not authorization.
7. Generated artifacts receive a stable document identifier and SHA-256 integrity reference.
8. Download, distribution, replacement/supersession, and archive are auditable actions.
9. Access follows domain/action authority: KAMTIB for Surat Izin Keluar Sementara operational flow; SUBBAG_TU for Surat Tugas Pengawalan administration within its authority; approver for approval/issue; auditor read/audit; Head Rudenim oversight/read-only.
10. Restricted health information is excluded unless explicitly authorized and contractually required; ordinary document templates must not leak PERKES data.

## Acceptance

Synthetic rendering tests verify required fields, version binding, numbering idempotency, authorization, hash stability, historical retrieval, download audit, and refusal of generation from an unapproved state.
