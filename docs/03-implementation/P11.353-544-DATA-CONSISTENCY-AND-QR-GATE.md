# P11.353–P11.544 — DATA CONSISTENCY & QR VALIDITY GATE

## Objective

Extend MTA DETENI from temporary-exit lifecycle consistency into cross-domain reconciliation without opening persistence or production access.

## P11.353–448 — Placement / Movement / Headcount reconciliation

The synthetic contract binds detainee identity, placement identity, movement events and expected versus observed headcount. Identity drift, malformed movement events, headcount mismatch and non-synthetic targets are blocked.

## P11.449–544 — Operational QR validity window

QR validity is evaluated against an explicit validity interval and scan timestamp. An operational QR is accepted only when its state is ACTIVE and the scan occurs within the interval. Context compatibility is explicit between RUDENIM_STAY, TEMPORARY_EXIT and DEPORTATION.

## Important semantic correction

Temporary-exit `COMPLETED` is not treated as a deportation event. Deportation remains a distinct operational context. The QR contract therefore does not infer deportation merely from completion of a temporary exit lifecycle.
