## Purpose

Define controlled placement of a detainee into block, room, and bed/slot while preserving temporal history, capacity constraints, authorization scope, and auditability.

## Rules

1. A placement is a time-bounded operational fact, not a mutable label.
2. A detainee may have at most one active placement for the applicable operational context.
3. A bed/slot may not have conflicting active occupants.
4. Transfer creates a new placement event and closes the prior active placement; history is retained.
5. Placement changes require authorized KAMTIB action and server-side scope checks.
6. Block/room/bed master data has explicit active/inactive lifecycle and cannot be silently reused in historical records.
7. Headcount projections derive from accepted placement/movement events; they are not an independent source of truth.
8. Invalid capacity, duplicate active placement, stale-state, or unauthorized transitions fail closed.
9. Every material change produces transactionally coupled audit evidence and an idempotency key/result.
10. Restricted PERKES information is not embedded into ordinary placement responses.

## Acceptance

Synthetic tests cover initial placement, transfer, duplicate-active conflict, occupied-slot conflict, unauthorized scope, stale version, closure, historical reconstruction, idempotent replay, and audit coupling.

## Dependency

`Core Administration → Placement → Movement & Headcount`.
