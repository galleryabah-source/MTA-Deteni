# P10.33 — Security Boundary Finalization

Status: PREPARED — synthetic/security contract.

## Non-negotiable invariants

1. Authentication and authorization are separate concerns.
2. Client claims are untrusted; effective authority is resolved server-side.
3. Deny-by-default applies to identity, permission, scope, duty, classification, resource, state, policy and SoD.
4. Super Admin cannot bypass operational authority requirements.
5. Sensitive resource resolution cannot disclose existence through authorization ordering.
6. Critical mutation is transactional, audited, and idempotent.
7. External provider work is post-commit only.
8. Artifact download requires a bound, expiring, atomic single-use grant.
9. Audit evidence is immutable/append-only and hash-verifiable.
10. AI remains assistive and OFF by default; it cannot become a critical transaction dependency.
11. Migration remains frozen until explicit governance approval and controlled DB verification.
12. Evidence must be independently observable before certification.

## Security completion meaning

P10.33 means the security boundaries are contractually consolidated. It does not mean production certification. Certification requires actual infrastructure evidence for the blockers listed in PROJECT_STATUS.md.
