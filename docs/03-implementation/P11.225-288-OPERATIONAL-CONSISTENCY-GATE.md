# P11.225–P11.288 — Operational Consistency Gate

## Objective

Connect placement, movement, headcount representation, detainee stay context and QR validity into one fail-closed synthetic contract.

## Checkpoint groups

- P11.225–P11.240: placement and movement identity;
- P11.241–P11.264: exited-state/headcount/temporary-exit QR consistency;
- P11.265–P11.280: return-state and Rudenim-stay QR consistency;
- P11.281–P11.288: target and negative-path enforcement.

## Invariants

- Headcount is a non-negative integer.
- An exited detainee cannot be accepted through `RUDENIM_STAY` QR context.
- A returned detainee must have an active `RUDENIM_STAY` QR context.
- Expired, future, inactive or context-mismatched QR states are not treated as operational acceptance.
- The contract is synthetic-only and does not authorize a live operational mutation.

## Boundary

This layer composes existing domain/application contracts; it does not replace authorization, audit/outbox, persistence, or the temporary-exit state machine.

## Safety

Migration Freeze TRUE; AI OFF; production unauthorized; no schema migration; no real detainee data.
