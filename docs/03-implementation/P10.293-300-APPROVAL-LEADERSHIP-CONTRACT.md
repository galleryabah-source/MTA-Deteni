## Approval

Approval is a distinct controlled action over a defined object and state. The system records actor, authority context, decision, timestamp, reason where required, version/state observed, and correlation reference. Approval cannot be performed by an incompatible actor or against a stale/revoked object.

## Leadership layer

Head Rudenim functions as substantive leadership oversight through PETUNJUK, ARAHAN, REKOMENDASI, and DISPOSISI. Oversight actions create auditable instructions/follow-up records and do not silently mutate operational facts.

## Separation of duties

- `SUPER_ADMIN`: authorization-policy governance only; not automatically substantive access to detainee data.
- `HEAD_RUDENIM`: leadership oversight; not automatically Super Admin and not ordinary operational editor.
- `KAMTIB`: operational temporary-exit/movement/escort execution within authority.
- `SUBBAG_TU`: administrative handling of escort assignment letters within authority.
- `RAP`: administrative detainee records and related workflows within authority.
- `PERKES`: restricted health domain within its authority.
- `AUDITOR`: audit/read access according to approved scope; no operational mutation.

## Guardrails

Critical approval/RBAC changes may require re-authentication and second approval according to policy. No actor may approve their own incompatible action where separation-of-duties rules prohibit it. Authorization is server-side and fail-closed.

## Acceptance

Synthetic tests cover valid approval, wrong role, wrong scope, stale object, self-approval conflict, revoked request, leadership direction lifecycle, acknowledgement/action/response/closure, audit evidence, and policy-cache refresh.
